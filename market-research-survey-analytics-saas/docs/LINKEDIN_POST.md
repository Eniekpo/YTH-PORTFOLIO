# LinkedIn project post draft

I built **InsightForge, a full-stack Market Research & Survey Analytics SaaS Platform** to demonstrate how I approach research technology beyond standalone dashboards.

The application lets a user create a research project, upload CSV or Excel survey responses, inspect data quality, explore response distributions, run cross-tabulations with chi-square tests, generate a statistically grounded research snapshot, and export analysis to Excel or PDF.

**Stack:** React, Vite, FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, SQLite, pandas, SciPy, Recharts, Docker and GitHub Actions.

A design choice I care about: the automated narrative is deterministic and evidence-based by default. It summarizes what the descriptive statistics support instead of presenting generated text as a substitute for analyst judgement.

This project demonstrates full-stack development, survey analytics, statistical interpretation, API design, data-quality thinking and research workflow automation in one application.
