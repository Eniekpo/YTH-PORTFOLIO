# Final Capstone: Executive Retail Performance Review

## Scenario

You are the analyst supporting a retail management team. They want a concise performance review of the synthetic 2024–2025 dataset.

## Deliverables

### Excel
- completed calculated columns
- KPI summary
- one regional or category chart
- three written observations

### SQL
Provide queries for:
- overall performance
- regional performance
- category performance
- monthly trend
- channel performance
- top products

### Python
- reproducible notebook
- data-quality checks
- grouped summaries
- at least three visualizations
- discount/profitability investigation

### Power BI
- executive overview
- product/channel page
- customer/sales page
- Year, Region, Category, and Channel slicers

### Executive Summary
Maximum one page:
- 3–5 key findings
- 3 recommendations
- at least 2 limitations or follow-up questions

## Analytical Standard

A strong submission:
- keeps KPI definitions consistent;
- distinguishes facts from interpretation;
- avoids unsupported causal claims;
- reconciles totals between tools;
- uses readable visualizations;
- connects analysis to a decision.
