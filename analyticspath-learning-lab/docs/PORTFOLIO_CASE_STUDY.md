# Portfolio Case Study

## Problem

Analytics learners often practise tools independently and struggle to understand how Excel, SQL, Python, and Power BI fit into one business workflow.

## Solution

AnalyticsPath Learning Lab uses one consistent retail dataset and one set of KPI definitions throughout the learning journey.

## Design

- **Excel:** foundational formulas and executive dashboard
- **SQL:** querying, aggregation, CASE logic, and window functions
- **Python:** reproducible EDA and visualization
- **Power BI:** modelling, DAX, and interactive reporting
- **Capstone:** executive synthesis and cross-tool reconciliation

## Portfolio Outcome

The project demonstrates analytics implementation, business KPI design, technical communication, training design, assessment design, reproducibility, analytical caution, and multi-tool workflow thinking.

## Data Disclosure

All records are synthetic and created solely for training and demonstration.
