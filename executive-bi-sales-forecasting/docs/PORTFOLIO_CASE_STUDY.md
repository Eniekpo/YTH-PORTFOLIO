# Portfolio Case Study

## Executive Business Intelligence & Sales Forecasting Platform

### The problem

Business leaders often receive fragmented reports from sales, finance, customer, and product teams. This makes it difficult to answer basic questions consistently: where growth is coming from, which products are profitable, which customers deserve attention, and what revenue may look like in the coming months.

### My solution

I built an end-to-end business intelligence platform that converts raw transaction data into a structured analytical database and presents decision-ready insights through an interactive executive dashboard.

The solution combines SQL data modelling, Python analytics, RFM customer segmentation, profitability analysis, revenue forecasting, and data-quality validation.

### Technical workflow

1. Generate a realistic synthetic commerce dataset.
2. Load customer, product, and transaction data into SQLite.
3. Model the data with fact and dimension tables.
4. Create SQL views for enriched sales analysis and monthly KPI reporting.
5. Calculate executive KPIs in Python.
6. Segment customers with RFM analysis.
7. Forecast monthly revenue using Holt-Winters exponential smoothing.
8. Surface all results in a filterable Streamlit dashboard.
9. Validate the pipeline with automated tests and data-quality checks.

### Business outcomes demonstrated

The application enables leaders to monitor revenue, gross profit, margins, orders, average order value, regional performance, channel mix, product profitability, customer value segments, returns, and future revenue expectations from one interface.

### Skills demonstrated

SQL, Python, pandas, SQLite, Streamlit, Plotly, statistics, time-series forecasting, customer analytics, data modelling, data quality, BI design, testing, and Docker.

### Data disclosure

The dataset is synthetic and was created specifically for portfolio demonstration. No client, employer, or confidential business information is used.
